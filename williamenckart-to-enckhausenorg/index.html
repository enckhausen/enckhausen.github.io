williamenck.art to enckhausen.com

William Enckhausen September 13, 2026

 

Please excuse the recent changes in the web address.  I have not had this blog very active for a while and it has been sitting on a data box that was hard to find and not visible in search engines.  I bought a domain, williamenck.art, for the blog.  However, I was hooked by an inexpensive price for the first year that later became very expensive.  I just wanted something simple and easy to remember and not too costly.  I did not want to pay so much for a boring philosophical blog without videos, photos, or junk to sell.  Therefore, I found a more generic .com domain:  enckhausen.com.  Even if the blog does not flourish again, I can keep the domain as it is very inexpensive and useful to help people find the blog.
